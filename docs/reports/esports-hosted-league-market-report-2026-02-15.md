# Hosted Esports League Platform Market Report (Global)
Date: February 15, 2026

## 1) Executive Summary
- **Estimated active recurring esports leagues worldwide (major titles):**
  - **Publicly discoverable:** **45,000 to 120,000** (base case: **75,000**)
  - **Private (schools, corporate, clubs, org-internal):** **90,000 to 420,000** (base case: **210,000**)
  - **Total:** **135,000 to 540,000** (base case: **285,000**)
- **Base-case monetization model (blended):** **$1,116 per league/year**
  - $900/year league SaaS fee (blended tier)
  - + premium user revenue (150 active users/league, 4% conversion, $3/month)
- **Base-case ARR potential from esports leagues only:**
  - **100% conversion of base-case market today:** **~$318.1M ARR**
  - At 2030 projected market size (6% annual market growth): **~$401.5M ARR**
  - 2030 sensitivity: **1% share = $4.0M ARR**, **5% = $20.1M**, **10% = $40.1M**
- **3-5 year realistic ramp (base case):** ~**$2.2M ARR (2026)** to ~**$18.1M ARR (2030)**.

## 2) Scope and Definitions
- Geography: worldwide, split by region.
- Included: recurring leagues with relatively stable player base across seasons.
- Excluded: one-off tournaments and pure ladders.
- Public league: publicly discoverable.
- Private league: not publicly discoverable (schools, corporate, clubs, semi-pro internal leagues).
- Game scope: major esports titles (including CS2, VALORANT, Apex Legends, Overwatch, StarCraft, plus other major esports ecosystems).

## 3) What Is Observable vs. What Is Inferred
There is no single global registry of recurring esports leagues. The estimate is a triangulation.

### 3.1 Observable anchors (sourced)
- Newzoo top game markets provide country-level player and revenue concentration (useful for regional weighting).
- Newzoo monthly-active rankings confirm the core major-title set in global PC/console engagement (CS2, LoL, Dota 2, VALORANT, Overwatch, Rocket League, etc.).
- Challengermode reports platform scale (tens of millions of users, ~80k organizers, ~400k competitions/month) and a paid organizer plan (99 EUR/month).
- Toornament reports 2023 activity: 6,400 new active organizers, 42,000 tournaments, >511,000 participants, 1.7M matches.
- PlayVS reports >100k active students and >10,500 teams in a Nintendo Switch school-league context.

### 3.2 Inference statements
- **Inference A:** Only a subset of competitive events are recurring leagues (vs one-off tournaments/ladders).
- **Inference B:** Private recurring leagues materially exceed public leagues due school/corporate/internal operations.
- **Inference C:** Regional mix of leagues broadly follows where player ecosystems are concentrated, then adjusted for private-league institutional adoption.

## 4) Sizing Method (Triangulated)
1. Build an active-league universe from platform throughput and organizer counts.
2. Convert event/competition scale into recurring-league equivalents.
3. Apply a public/private split (private favored for school/corporate/internal use cases).
4. Allocate by region using market/player concentration anchors.
5. Apply monetization model to compute TAM/SAM/SOM and ARR ramp.

## 6) Revenue Model and ARR

### 6.1 Acronyms (quick definitions)
- **TAM**: Total Addressable Market (if you could sell to the full market).
- **SAM**: Serviceable Addressable Market (portion you can serve with current product/coverage).
- **SOM**: Serviceable Obtainable Market (realistically capturable share in your time horizon).

### 6.2 Pricing assumptions (base case)
- League SaaS fee (blended): **$900/year per league**.
- Premium user plan: **$3/month**, **4% conversion**, **150 active users/league**.
- Premium revenue per league/year: `150 * 0.04 * 3 * 12 = $216`.
- **Total blended revenue per league/year: $1,116**.

Formula:
`ARR = leagues_converted * (league_fee + active_users_per_league * conversion_rate * premium_monthly * 12)`

### 6.3 TAM ARR (esports leagues only)
Using base-case league count (285,000):
- **Base pricing TAM ARR now:** `285,000 * $1,116 = ~$318.1M`
- With 6% annual league growth, 2030 market ~359,806 leagues:
  - **2030 TAM ARR:** `359,806 * $1,116 = ~$401.5M`

### 6.6 Example TAM/SAM/SOM framing (base case)
- **TAM (2026):** 285,000 leagues -> **$318.1M ARR** at full conversion.
- **SAM (2026):** assume initial product/service coverage can address 60% of TAM (major titles + reachable geos + support constraints) -> 171,000 leagues -> **$190.8M ARR** at full SAM conversion.

## 7) What This Implies for Your Current Pricing Signal
Your datapoint (5k total users / 1.2k active users / hosting floor near $40/mo and current cost of $180/mo) supports a hosted pricing tier around **$1,500/year** for large leagues.

If you keep a tiered model (small/medium/large leagues) and land a blended $900-$1,200 annual league fee, the premium-user layer can add **~20%-80% uplift** depending conversion and activity density.

## 8) Expansion Beyond Esports Into Physical Sports Leagues/Orgs

### 8.1 Adjacent proof that this buyer type pays
- SportsEngine reports >16M athletes, 1.2M teams, and 45,000 organizations using its platform.
- TeamSnap reports >30M users and 19,000+ sports organizations.
- GameChanger reports very large youth-sports usage in 2024 (millions of games/teams/users).

### 8.2 Expansion implication (inference)
- School/club/semi-pro workflows are structurally similar to esports league ops (registration, scheduling, standings, comms, monetization).
- A practical adjacent-serviceable market for schools/clubs/semi-pro orgs can exceed esports-league-only market over time.

### 8.3 Example expansion ARR scenarios (adjacent physical orgs)

## 9) Confidence and Gaps
- Confidence is **moderate** on directional size and monetization range.
- Confidence is **low-moderate** on exact global league counts because no central ledger exists.
- Largest uncertainty drivers:
  - recurring-league vs one-off competition ratio,
  - private/public split by region,
  - true active users per league and premium conversion by title/region.

This report excludes one-off tournaments by design. If useful, next pass can size this separately from recurring leagues so it does not distort league ARR assumptions.
